Privacy Policy Effective Date: July 2, 2026 CornerStamp is developed and published by 102223629 Saskatchewan Ltd. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how CornerStamp handles your information. Information We Collect CornerStamp does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information. The app does not require user accounts, registration, or sign-in. Photos Photos taken with CornerStamp are stored only on your device and in your personal photo library if you choose to save them. The developer does not have access to your photos. Location Information CornerStamp requests access to your device's precise location solely to display location information on your photos. Location data: Is processed only on your device. Is never transmitted to our servers. Is never shared with third parties. Is only used while creating location-stamped photos. Notes Any notes you enter are stored only within the saved image's watermark or metadata on your device. These notes are never transmitted to the developer. Apple Maps CornerStamp uses Apple Maps to display map information within the app. Use of Apple Maps is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. Internet Connectivity CornerStamp does not upload your photos, notes, GPS coordinates, or any other personal information to the internet. The app does not operate any backend servers that receive user data. Analytics and Crash Reporting CornerStamp does not use: Analytics services Advertising services Crash reporting services User tracking technologies Advertising CornerStamp does not display advertisements. In-App Purchases CornerStamp does not include subscriptions or in-app purchases. Children's Privacy CornerStamp is suitable for users aged 4 years and older. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. Permissions CornerStamp requests the following permissions: Camera Used to capture photos. Location Used to determine your current location so it can be included on your photos. Photo Library Used only to save completed photos to your device. These permissions are used solely for the features of the app and are never used to collect information for the developer.
